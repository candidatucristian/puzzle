## Antique carpet

- The Metropolitan Museum of Art, **Carpet**, Iran, dated 1280 AH / 1863 CE.
- Object number **57.5**, Gift of Ann Payne Blumenthal, 1957.
- Collection record and Public Domain designation: <https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/451375>
- Original photograph: <https://collectionapi.metmuseum.org/api/collection/v1/iiif/451375/899962/main-image>
- Downloaded 2026-10-22 as `met-carpet-57-5.jpg`.
- `persian-carpet-color.jpg`: rectangular interior crop (42,60,844,1080), rotated 90 degrees. Used only as the albedo of the modeled carpet; thickness, weave, ripples, shading and perspective are rendered by Cycles.
- The Met Open Access permits unrestricted commercial and noncommercial use of its public-domain images: <https://www.metmuseum.org/about-the-met/policies-and-documents/open-access>
