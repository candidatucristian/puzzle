# Antique toy materials

`public/assets/images/Cot/antique-toy-materials-v1.webp` is an original bitmap material atlas generated with the built-in imagegen tool, then encoded as WebP. It supplies colour and fine surface relief to the five true 3D cot toys. The geometry, light, shadows and animation remain live Three.js objects.

The nine cells, in row order: walnut, burgundy bookcloth, paper, aged brass, charcoal felt, cream painted wood, roof tiles, blue lining, grey wool. Runtime cropping samples inside each cell to avoid grid seams. Colour textures use sRGB; relief maps use linear luminance and a sub-millimetre bump scale.

## Generation prompt

Use case: product-mockup.
Asset type: photorealistic albedo texture atlas for tiny antique 3D toys in a dark Edwardian nursery game.
Create ONE square image divided EXACTLY into a 3 by 3 grid of nine equal square flat material swatches. No gaps, frames, margins, labels, objects, perspective, cast shadows, gloss highlights or text. Every cell is a straight-on macro material photograph, fills its whole square, neutral diffuse even illumination; physically plausible finely detailed surface, subdued natural tones, slight age and tiny wear, not distressed ruins.
Grid row 1 left to right: (1) dark warm walnut wood, tightly flowing fine lengthwise grain, mellow old shellac, no plank seams; (2) faded oxblood burgundy bookbinder cloth, very fine woven linen, slight worn lighter fibres; (3) warm aged ivory book paper, subtle fibre, tiny foxing, no writing.
Grid row 2 left to right: (4) aged warm brass, fine hairline scratches, muted tarnish; (5) matte charcoal black wool felt, fine soft fibres, almost black; (6) old warm cream painted wood for a toy building, very subtle brushed paint, small delicate paint wear.
Grid row 3 left to right: (7) terracotta miniature roof tiles, regular small overlapping rows of old reddish-brown rounded shingles, flat texture photograph, no roof silhouette; (8) dusty desaturated blue satin lining, tiny fine textile weave and very gentle soft wrinkles, no strong lighting; (9) dark warm grey wool trouser fabric, fine tight weave and subtle nap.
Keep borders aligned at exactly one third and two thirds. Photographic natural material realism, enough fine detail to withstand close view, not cartoon, no illustrations, no CGI render, no watermark.
