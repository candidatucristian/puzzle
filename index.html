How to Play

◆

THE GOAL

Every level hides a secret word — the code. Explore the scene: look, listen, count, experiment. When you think you have it, type it in the code box at the bottom of the screen and press EXECUTE (or hit Enter). A wrong code just flashes red — guessing costs nothing, but the answer is always earned.

THE CONTROLS

EXECUTE

Submits the code you typed in the box next to it.

⟳

Restarts the current level from scratch.

HINT

Three hints for each room, revealed one at a time. Start with a direction and ask for more only when you need it.

INSPECT

Get closer to letters and small objects. Drag to look around, use + and − to zoom, then press Escape (or Back to play) to return.

☰ · LEVELS

On a phone or tablet the menu and the levels panel slide in from the sides. Tap what you would click; drag what you would drag. The rooms want the phone held sideways.

⛶

Slides the top bar away and asks the browser for full screen, so the room fills the phone; the code box stays. The bar also has a small handle on its edge: tap it, or pull it up or down, to slide the bar away or back. Safari on iPhone has no full screen at all: to lose its bars, tap Share, choose Add to Home Screen, and open the game from that icon.

🔊

Game volume. Drag the slider, or click the icon to mute and unmute.

Options

Music and sound-effect volume settings. This is also where you can reset the game — it wipes all progress and starts over from Level 1 (it asks you to confirm first).

THE LEVELS PANEL

2

The level you are playing right now.

1

A solved level — click it any time to revisit.

🔒

Still locked. Levels open in order — solve the current one to unlock the next.

THE BADGES

Open HINT and you may find badges under the title. They tell you what kind of attention the level wants:

LISTENING

Audio matters here. Turn the volume up and listen carefully — something is said only in sound. On an iPhone, the ring/silent switch must be set to ring for the game to be heard.

REFERENCE

The level speaks a language you may need to look up: an alphabet, a cipher, a notation. Decoding it is the puzzle.

ONE LAST THING

Everything you need is inside the room. Nothing is random — if something feels slightly wrong, it probably is. That is the way in.

A little guidance

◆

Take one hint at a time. There is no penalty for asking.
