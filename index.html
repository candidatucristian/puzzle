              How to play

              Find the answer

              Each room hides an answer. Look, listen and interact with the scene. Enter what you find in the answer field below the room. Wrong answers carry no penalty.

              The controls

              Submit
              Check your answer, or press Enter.
              Restart
              The circular arrow beside Submit starts the current room again. Your progress is kept.
              Hint
              Use Hint below the room to reveal one clue at a time.
              Keys
              M the map of the house · N your notebook · C the case file · Z the magnifying glass, or hold the right mouse button · Esc back the way you came.
              Settings
              Adjust sound, text size, motion and effects. Reset game clears all progress after a second click to confirm.

              Moving between rooms

              Solve a room to unlock the next. Revisit unlocked rooms from the list on the right or use the arrows above the scene. Progress is saved automatically in this browser.

              Clue labels

              Listening
              Sound is part of the puzzle. Check the volume in Settings and listen carefully.
              Reference may help
              You may need to look up an alphabet, cipher or notation to decode the clue.

              Phone and tablet browsers

              Ashcombe Manor is available on desktop and laptop browsers. Open it on a computer to play.
