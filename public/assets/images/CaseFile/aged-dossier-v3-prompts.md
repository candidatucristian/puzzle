## Packaging and placement

`.artifacts/casefile-redesign/prepare-aged-v3.mjs` crops the exterior matting/padding, preserving the photographic materials, and converts to WebP with quality 92 / alpha quality 100. The final sizes are 1173 × 1159 (paper), 635 × 1956 (index leaf), and 368 × 1495 (steel).

`src/ui/styles/casefile-materials.css` composes separate paper leaves over the existing leather, gives the leaves thin offset backing layers, and reserves the center channel for the steel rail. The metal is decorative and cannot capture clicks or keyboard focus. Text, tab controls and document metadata remain on the actual reading surfaces.
