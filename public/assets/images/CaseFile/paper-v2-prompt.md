## Prompt

Use case: product-mockup.
Asset type: single isolated blank letter paper texture for an interactive Victorian manor mystery game. Only the physical paper is the image; live text and red wax seal are added separately by the app.
Primary request: an extraordinarily REAL, photographic sheet of vintage correspondence stationery, not a drawing, parchment, map or painted background.
Composition: straight orthographic overhead view, one portrait rectangular sheet of thin warm ivory cotton writing paper, approximately A-series proportions. The sheet occupies almost all the canvas, with a small 2% transparent margin on every side. Full sheet visible. Straight mostly parallel edges, minute natural edge irregularities, not ragged or scalloped.
Materials: fine matte paper fibers visible at close inspection, delicate real paper grain, subtle translucency at the thinnest edges. Two subtle horizontal fold creases located exactly at one-third and two-thirds of the sheet height, as a letter folded into three panels and opened again. No vertical center fold. Tiny soft uneven buckling alongside the folds. One very small lifted lower right corner revealing the thin underside, not a big rolled scroll. Soft directional illumination from upper left giving believable tiny highlights and self-shadow along the folds. Restrained natural aging at edges; center is clean pale warm ivory with very little tonal variation so black text remains perfectly readable.
Constraints: true transparent alpha background, no table, no scene, no hands, no letters or writing, no ink, no seals or decorations, no dark burned border, no large tears, no stains, no mottled brown grunge. No cast shadow beyond the paper; application adds its own shadow. Real archival macro product photography, crisp fine physical details, no painted brushwork, no illustration.
