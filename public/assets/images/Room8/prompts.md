# Room VIII — the weighing room: photo assets to generate

Generate each with the built-in imagegen tool, as the Room VII assets were
(see ../Room7/prompts.md), with true alpha transparency. Save them in this
folder under the file names given. Everything that moves or carries the
puzzle — the beam's angle, what stands on the pans, the water, the chalk
writing, the litmus — stays painted live in code over or inside these, so
each asset must be **empty** of it, exactly as described.

Until these exist, the room and the three levels are painted in code.

---

## balance-stand.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: transparent game prop cutout. The fixed stand of an antique 1900s apothecary two-pan balance, seen straight from the front at eye level: a dark mahogany plinth with a moulded top edge, one small drawer with a brass knob, two brass levelling feet; rising from its middle a slender fluted brass column with turned collars at the foot and near the top, ending in a small brass knife-edge bearing block. Low on the column a small ivory scale plate with fine black division ticks and a longer zero tick in the middle. Two short brass stop posts standing on the plinth to left and right. NO beam, NO pans, NO chains, NO pointer — the column top is bare. Warm worn brass with real patina and fine scratches, polished where hands touched it. Soft warm light from upper left. Centered, all edges visible, 4 percent transparent margin. No background, no table, no text other than the scale ticks. Output portrait 1024x1536.
