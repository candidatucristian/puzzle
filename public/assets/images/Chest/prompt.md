# Gold chest material

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool as one two-state transparent atlas: [gold-chest-states.png](./gold-chest-states.png). Original alpha is preserved. The game registers the body and lid onto the existing projected chest dimensions; shadows, key, interaction and key animation remain live.

## Prompt

Use case: product-mockup.
Asset type: one transparent game sprite atlas with exactly two states of ONE identical antique golden treasure chest, shown side by side.
Primary request: make the small chest look like an actual real gold coffer, photographed, with natural polished gold reflections, fine tool marks and tiny wear. It belongs on a desk in a dark English manor.
Layout: wide canvas, two equal square cells side by side, transparent everywhere outside the objects. LEFT cell: chest shut. RIGHT cell: THE SAME chest open, lid rotated back to an upright position, its empty interior and deep burgundy velvet lining visible. In both cells, the BODY of the chest must be the same size and sit at exactly the same bottom baseline; camera, body, feet, perspective, light and lock stay fixed. Only lid position changes. No captions, no letters.
Camera: near frontal shallow three-quarter view from a little ABOVE and slightly LEFT of the front, long front face nearly horizontal and facing viewer, narrow LEFT side visible, shallow top visible. NOT right-side view, not isometric. Orthographic product photography, no lens distortion.
Object: low rectangular small casket about 25cm wide, 15cm deep, 8cm tall body, gently barrel-vaulted lid another 5cm high. Entire exterior rich real metallic gold, no exposed wood, no gems. Subtle hand engraved floral chasing, two raised gold straps over the lid at 20% and 80% width, round tiny rivets. Central front gold lock plate with black keyhole. Four very small rounded gold feet. The shape is restrained and believable, solid expensive antique, not fantasy pirate loot. Burgundy velvet lining inside lid and cavity of open chest. Open chest completely empty: NO key, no coins, no treasure and no magical light.
Lighting: soft warm light from upper right, restrained sharp gold highlights, darker warm brown reflective shadows on the left side, softly lit visible details in open lid and rim. No bloom, no floating sparkles, no painted gradients, no plastic yellow. Photoreal museum object photography with a little natural age.
Constraints: preserve full object silhouette in each cell with generous clear transparent margin above and narrow margin at sides, genuine transparent alpha background, NO table, NO background, NO scenery, no cast shadow outside the feet. The app renders the key, shadows, animation and scene separately.
