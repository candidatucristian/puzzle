## Exact prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: 2-by-2 square texture atlas for a genuinely three-dimensional Victorian manor staircase in a dark mystery game.
Primary request: Four clean, physically plausible photographic material scans, orthographic, flat and evenly lit, each filling exactly one quadrant of a square atlas. No borders or gutters. No staircase scene, no objects, no text.
Top left quadrant: old polished English oak, medium warm walnut-brown, fine dense wood grain running HORIZONTALLY left to right, subtle age, tiny pores, delicate worn satin varnish, a few gentle scratches, absolutely no floorboard seams.
Top right quadrant: dark charcoal olive-grey lime plaster, fine mottled tactile mineral surface, small natural imperfections, well-maintained old interior, no crumbling, no damp stains, no large cracks.
Bottom left quadrant: deeper brown antique walnut joinery, fine horizontal grain, restrained dark satin finish, slight authentic wear, no panel seams.
Bottom right quadrant: dark blackened iron, very fine matte forged texture, no rust patches.
Style/medium: actual close-up architectural conservation material photography, extremely realistic, crisp tiny details at natural scale, no illustration, no painted strokes, no plastic CGI.
Lighting: neutral soft diffuse scan lighting; no cast shadows, no vignettes, no directional reflections, no baked lighting. Keep sufficient midtone detail so the game can darken these scans with its own light.
Composition: precisely equal four square quadrants, seamless-looking material patches, square final image. Materials only.
