# His Cot (Room VI): photographic assets

The level works now with everything painted in code (`src/levels/cot/art.js`, `inRoom.js`).
These photographs would replace the painted cot and its things. What moves stays
drawn in code over them: the dancers on the music box (they turn), Chaplin's
bowler (he raises it), the hotel's lit windows, the mobile's felt shapes.
The only writing anywhere is the title on the book, and it is drawn in code
too, so the book's cover in the photograph must be BLANK.

Save as PNG with real transparency (no chequer baked in):

- `cot-room-v1.png`: the cot for the Room VI photograph
- `cot-closeup-v1.png`: the close-up from above, with his other things but without the five
- `cot-five-v1.png`: the five things, each separate

---

## 1. `cot-room-v1.png`: the cot in Room VI

Use case: product cutout for compositing into an existing photograph.
Asset: transparent background (RGBA), portrait 1024x1280.
A real 1950s English baby cot of turned wood, painted a soft sage green, slightly worn: four corner posts with round knobs, a top rail and a lower rail on each side, rounded bars between them. A curved painted mobile arm rising from the back left post and over the middle of the cot, a small wooden ring hanging from it with five felt shapes on threads (two stars, a crescent moon, two clouds). Inside, a white sheet printed with small blue moons and stars, a pillow at the head; nothing else visible from this distance.
Camera: eye level about 138 cm, the cot almost square-on with its long side to the camera, seen a little from the right of the room's centre, so that only a sliver of the mattress is seen over the front rail.
Lighting: night. Cool moonlight from a tall window off to the LEFT, faint warm light from far left, everything else in deep shadow. No cast shadow on any floor or wall (the game adds the contact shadow).

## 2. `cot-closeup-v1.png`: looking down into the cot

Use case: game background. Asset: landscape 1792x1024, photograph.
Looking down into the same sage-painted cot at night, from above its front rail at about 45 degrees. The front rail across the bottom, close and soft; the two side rails running back on the left and right; the back rail with its rounded bars; behind it a deep teal wall with felt clouds and felt stars pinned to it and a small gilt oval frame with a pressed red rose. The mobile on its arm from the back left post, its wooden ring above the middle, its felt shapes REMOVED (the game hangs them).
On the mattress, a white sheet printed with small blue moons and stars; his flat pillow at the back left with a knitted rabbit sitting in the corner beside it; a pair of pale blue knitted bootees and a teddy bear lying on his back near the back rail in the middle; a wooden ring rattle near the front left; an oatmeal knitted blanket folded at the front right corner.
These five places must be EMPTY sheet, for the game's five things: left middle, left of centre near the back, centre front, right of centre near the back, right middle.
Lighting: night, cool moonlight from the upper LEFT, a warm night-light glow from the right, deep shadows at the edges. Photographic, no baby, no electronics.

## 3. `cot-five-v1.png`: the five things

Use case: product cutouts, one sheet.
Asset: transparent background (RGBA), landscape 2000x1000, five objects in one row with clear space between them, each photographed from about 40 degrees above, in the same dim light: cool moonlight from the upper left, a faint warm fill from the right. No cast shadows on any surface.
Left to right:
1. A small antique walnut music box, set down turned a little to the right, its lid open and standing up behind it, lined with pale blue satin and a small oval mirror; a small round brass stage on its top, EMPTY; a tiny painted spray of flowers on its front; a brass winding key at its side.
2. A small replica of the Oscar statuette: the gold knight with crossed arms and sword standing on a film reel, on a black base with a gold band. About 17 cm tall.
3. A small closed book lying flat, cover up, turned a little: red cloth binding with a gilt double border, completely BLANK (no title), cream page edges, a green ribbon bookmark trailing out.
4. A painted wooden figure of Charlie Chaplin standing: big black shoes turned out, baggy grey trousers, tight black coat, white shirt and bow tie, white face, small black moustache, cane planted in his right hand, and WITHOUT his bowler hat (the game adds it).
5. A wooden toy hotel, set down turned a little to the left so its right side shows: three floors painted cream, rows of windows with blue shutters, a striped red-and-white awning over a green arched door, a tiny brass bell beside the door, a red tiled roof, a brick chimney, a small green flag. No sign, no text.
Photographic, real materials, gently worn. No text, letters or numbers anywhere.
